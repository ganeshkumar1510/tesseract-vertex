**Tesseract VERTEX | Open-Source Freelance CRM**

Building business software should not require freelancers to commit to another recurring subscription.

Tesseract VERTEX is an open-source CRM designed for freelancers and small teams to manage clients, sales pipelines, projects, tasks, invoices, and business reporting from one workspace.

The product follows a dual-access model: users can sign up for the cloud-hosted offering or self-host their own instance for greater control and extensive customization.

**Key capabilities**
- Client and relationship management
- Visual project and sales pipeline management
- Task and project tracking
- Invoice generation
- Business analytics and reporting
- Cloud access and self-hosted deployment

**Product vision:** Make professional business management accessible to independent professionals through software that is free, open source, and adaptable to their needs.

**Core principle:** Open source. Forever free. Your business, your way.
